# Discovery questions: <Client>

Ask in order. Stop a thread when you reach a number, a name, or a date.

## Sponsor (30 min)
1. What outcome would make this worth the investment this quarter?
2. If we do nothing for six months, what does it cost?
3. What number would you show your leadership on Day 30?
4. Who must agree for this to go to production?
5. Who will own this after we leave?

## Users (45 min, watch them work)
1. Walk me through the last time you did <task>. Where did time go?
2. What do you check, copy, or re-type by hand?
3. Where does the information you trust come from?
4. What would make you stop using a new tool after a week?

## IT / platform
1. How do new systems get network access and identities? Typical lead time?
2. Which environments can we use (dev/test/prod), and who approves promotion?
3. What would you need to support this on Day 2?

## Data owners
1. Which system is the source of truth for <entity>?
2. Can we get a sample or profile this week? Under what conditions?
3. What do the status codes / free-text fields actually mean?

## Security / compliance
1. What classification does this data carry?
2. Which reviews apply (security architecture, privacy, vendor, change board)? Lead times?
3. Which vendors and subprocessors are already approved?
4. What evidence will you need from us?
